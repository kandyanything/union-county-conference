    About the Union County Conference

    The Union County Conference (UCC) is comprised of 22 public and private high schools located in Union County, New Jersey. The conference is dedicated to promoting excellence in athletics, fostering sportsmanship, and providing opportunities for student-athletes to compete at the highest level.

    All Union County Conference member schools are members of the New Jersey State Interscholastic Athletic Association (NJSIAA) and compete in accordance with NJSIAA rules and regulations.

    Conference Mission

    The Union County Conference strives to:

    • Promote fair competition and sportsmanship among member schools
    • Provide opportunities for student-athletes to develop their athletic skills
    • Foster positive relationships between schools and communities
    • Support the educational mission of our member schools through athletics
    • Maintain high standards of integrity and ethical conduct in all athletic programs
