    About Us

    Our Conference

    The Union County Conference is comprised of 22 public and private high schools in Union County in New Jersey. Our member schools are dedicated to promoting excellence in athletics and sportsmanship.

    The conference strives to promote fair competition among member schools, provide opportunities for student-athletes to develop their skills, foster positive relationships between schools and communities, and maintain high standards of integrity in all athletic programs.

    All Union County Conference member schools belong to the New Jersey State Interscholastic Athletic Association (NJSIAA) and compete in accordance with NJSIAA rules and regulations.
